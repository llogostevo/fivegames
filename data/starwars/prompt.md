I am building a geography-based daily quiz game called **Pin5**.

I already have other Pin5 datasets, including an Airports game. I now want you to create a new dataset:

# Pin5: Star Wars

This is a **public entertainment-geography quiz** about real-world places connected to the Star Wars franchise.

The player already knows they are playing a **Star Wars quiz**.

The objective is NOT to identify a fictional planet, character, or film title alone.

The objective is:

**Identify a real-world public place connected to Star Wars — a filming location, studio, theme-park land, premiere venue, exhibition site, or similar — from five progressively easier clues, then place a pin on the map as close as possible to that real place.**

Every map target must be a **real Earth location** that a general-audience quiz player can pin on a map.

Fictional places (Tatooine, Hoth, Endor, Coruscant, etc.) may appear in clues as Star Wars knowledge, but they are **never** the map target. The pin always goes on the real-world place (the Tunisian desert used for Tatooine, the Norwegian glacier used for Hoth, Galaxy's Edge at a specific Disney park, and so on).

There is **no fixed target number of locations**.

Build the largest high-quality Star Wars Pin5 dataset that can reasonably be created from well-documented, meaningful geographical connections to **public / documented production and franchise places**.

Quantity must NEVER come at the expense of quality.

---

# 1. CORE GAME MECHANIC

Every location has exactly five clues.

The player starts with Clue 1 and can either guess the location on the map or reveal another clue.

Each clue should make the answer progressively easier.

The established Pin5 clue structure for this game is:

### Clue 1 — Hard Star Wars fact

A difficult fact aimed at knowledgeable Star Wars fans.

It should have a genuine connection to the real-world target location.

It should be interesting and specific rather than generic.

A serious fan might identify the place from this clue, but most players should need more information.

### Clue 2 — Easier Star Wars fact / famous association

Give another Star Wars-specific fact that makes the location easier to identify.

This should reveal meaningfully more information than Clue 1.

### Clue 3 — Strong narrowing clue

Give a clue that significantly narrows the answer.

This could identify or strongly indicate:

- film or TV series
- trilogy / era (Original, Prequel, Sequel, Anthology, TV)
- famous in-universe place portrayed there
- production company / studio
- theme park / attraction
- premiere or exhibition
- city or country
- well-known franchise landmark

A knowledgeable Star Wars fan should now have a strong chance of identifying the real-world location.

### Clue 4 — Explicit answer

State the exact real-world location.

For example:

"The location is Hotel Sidi Driss."

"The location is Hardangerjøkulen glacier."

"The location is Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge at Disneyland."

Clue 4 MUST explicitly identify the real-world answer.

### Clue 5 — Geographical locating clue

Now help the player physically find the answer on the map.

For example:

"The location is in Matmata, southern Tunisia."

"The location is on the Hardanger Plateau in Vestland, Norway."

"The location is in Anaheim, California, United States."

Clue 5 should make the geography easier but MUST NOT provide coordinates.

---

# 2. IMPORTANT: THIS IS ALREADY A STAR WARS GAME

Do NOT waste clues establishing that Star Wars is involved.

Bad clues include:

"This location is associated with a famous space fantasy franchise."

"A well-known sci-fi film was shot here."

"This place has a connection to Star Wars."

The player already knows that.

Every clue should provide useful information about **why this particular real-world place is connected to Star Wars**.

Also:

Do NOT make the player pin a fictional planet. If Clue 4 says "The location is Tatooine," that is wrong. Clue 4 must name the real Earth place.

# 3. WHAT COUNTS AS A VALID LOCATION?

Search the Star Wars franchise's **real-world production and public-franchise geography** for meaningful places.

Focus on entertainment-geography categories such as filming locations, studios, theme parks, premiere venues, exhibitions, and other well-documented public franchise sites.

Potential categories include:

## Film and TV filming locations

Locations used for principal photography, second unit, or other documented filming across:

- Original Trilogy
- Prequel Trilogy
- Sequel Trilogy
- Anthology / standalone films (*Rogue One*, *Solo*, etc.)
- Live-action series (*The Mandalorian*, *Andor*, *Obi-Wan Kenobi*, *Ahsoka*, etc.)
- other officially produced live-action Star Wars projects with documented real-world shoots

Potential targets include:

- deserts, canyons, forests, glaciers, beaches, islands
- towns, villages, historic sites
- temples, ruins, castles, plazas
- hotels and buildings used as sets
- specific named natural landmarks
- studio backlots only where they are a meaningful, documentable, pin-able place

However:

**Do NOT automatically create a question for every vaguely similar landscape.**

The place must have a documented Star Wars filming (or production) connection strong enough to support a good Pin5 question.

Prefer the most specific documented site (a named hotel, temple, glacier, plaza) over a whole country or generic desert.

---

## Studios and production facilities

Include well-documented commercial / public-industry places such as:

- film studios used for Star Wars productions
- sound stages or studio complexes with a clear Star Wars connection
- visual-effects / production campuses where the public connection is well documented (e.g. historic ILM / Lucasfilm associations)

Only include these where the Star Wars connection is meaningful enough for a fan quiz.

---

## Theme parks and attractions

Include official Star Wars theme-park lands and attractions where the real-world site is pin-able, such as:

- Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (Disneyland, Disney's Hollywood Studios, and any other official parks)
- Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser (where historically relevant and documentable)
- other official Disney / Lucasfilm Star Wars attractions with a clear physical location

Treat each park's Galaxy's Edge (or equivalent) as a distinct location if coordinates and clues can meaningfully differ.

---

## Premieres, exhibitions and public franchise events

Include places associated with:

- world / major premieres
- significant public exhibitions
- museum displays of Star Wars artefacts or costumes (where the venue connection is strong)
- official celebrations or franchise landmarks open to the public

Only include them where the geographical location is meaningful and the Star Wars connection can support five good clues.

---

## Franchise landmarks and other public places

Other categories are permitted if the connection is:

- significant
- interesting
- geographically meaningful
- well documented
- suitable for a Star Wars fan quiz

Examples might include Skywalker Ranch visitor-facing / publicly documented landmarks only where appropriate for a general-audience quiz, official visitor centres, or widely documented public franchise sites.

Do not add tenuous locations merely to increase the dataset size.

Every map target must be a real place suitable for inclusion in a general-audience geography quiz.

# 4. TARGET THE MOST INTERESTING GEOGRAPHICAL LEVEL

Always choose the most specific sensible map target.

For example:

Prefer:

**Hotel Sidi Driss**

**Tunisia**

**Whippendell Woods**

**England**

**Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge at Disney's Hollywood Studios**

rather than:

**Orlando**

when the clues are about that specific land.

However, cities, towns, islands, regions, states or countries are acceptable where that genuinely represents the meaningful Star Wars connection.

The target must correspond to where the player should actually place their pin — always on Earth.

# 5. AVOID DUPLICATE MAP TARGETS

I want as many **distinct real-world locations** as the evidence supports — not many pieces of Star Wars trivia attached to a small number of regions.

Do not repeatedly use:

- Tunisia
- London
- Los Angeles
- Orlando
- etc.

as generic targets.

Different specific locations within the same country or city ARE acceptable.

For example, several Tunisian locations could be included if each has:

- a distinct physical location;
- its own coordinates;
- a meaningful Star Wars connection;
- substantially different clues.

Likewise, multiple UK studio / woodland / plaza locations are acceptable if they are genuinely separate places.

Before writing the final dataset, deduplicate all coordinates and location identities.

If the target is a street or neighbourhood, choose an appropriate representative point relevant to the Star Wars connection.

If the target genuinely is a city, an appropriate central coordinate may be used.

# 9. CLUE QUALITY

The clues are one of the most important parts of the dataset.

Avoid boring generic clues such as:

"Star Wars was filmed here."

"This is a desert used in a movie."

"This location is in Africa."

"A famous sci-fi franchise shot scenes here."

Instead use interesting Star Wars-specific facts.

Clue 1 and Clue 2 should reward Star Wars knowledge (films, scenes, in-universe places, production trivia).

Clue 3 should significantly narrow the possibilities.

Clue 4 gives the real-world answer.

Clue 5 helps locate that answer geographically.

The progression should feel like:

**Star Wars deep cut → recognisable franchise fact → strong narrowing clue → real-world answer → map help**

Each clue must add new information.

Do not simply reword the same fact five times.

A strong pattern often looks like:

1. obscure production / scene detail
2. clearer in-universe place or famous scene
3. film / series / park / city narrowing
4. explicit real-world place name
5. country / region locating help
