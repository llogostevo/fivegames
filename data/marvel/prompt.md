I am building a geography-based daily quiz game called **Pin5**.

I already have other Pin5 datasets, including an Airports game. I now want you to create a new dataset:

# Pin5: Marvel

This is a **public entertainment-geography quiz** about real-world places connected to the Marvel franchise (primarily the Marvel Cinematic Universe, plus other well-documented Marvel film / TV / park geography).

The player already knows they are playing a **Marvel quiz**.

The objective is NOT to identify a fictional city, character, or film title alone.

The objective is:

**Identify a real-world public place connected to Marvel — a filming location, studio, theme-park land, landmark used on screen, premiere venue, exhibition site, or similar — from five progressively easier clues, then place a pin on the map as close as possible to that real place.**

Every map target must be a **real Earth location** that a general-audience quiz player can pin on a map.

Fictional places (Asgard, Wakanda, Sokovia, Knowhere, the Sanctum Sanctorum as a fictional address, etc.) may appear in clues as Marvel knowledge, but they are **never** the map target. The pin always goes on the real-world place (the Atlanta / Georgia sites used for Wakanda scenes, Pinewood / Shepperton / Trilith studios, Avengers Campus at a specific Disney park, New York landmarks used for MCU street scenes, and so on).

There is **no fixed target number of locations**.

Build the largest high-quality Marvel Pin5 dataset that can reasonably be created from well-documented, meaningful geographical connections to **public / documented production and franchise places**.

Quantity must NEVER come at the expense of quality.

---

# 1. CORE GAME MECHANIC

Every location has exactly five clues.

The player starts with Clue 1 and can either guess the location on the map or reveal another clue.

Each clue should make the answer progressively easier.

The established Pin5 clue structure for this game is:

### Clue 1 — Hard Marvel fact

A difficult fact aimed at knowledgeable Marvel fans.

It should have a genuine connection to the real-world target location.

It should be interesting and specific rather than generic.

A serious fan might identify the place from this clue, but most players should need more information.

### Clue 2 — Easier Marvel fact / famous association

Give another Marvel-specific fact that makes the location easier to identify.

This should reveal meaningfully more information than Clue 1.

### Clue 3 — Strong narrowing clue

Give a clue that significantly narrows the answer.

This could identify or strongly indicate:

- film or TV series
- MCU Phase / era
- famous in-universe place portrayed there
- studio / production hub
- theme park / attraction
- premiere or exhibition
- city or country
- well-known franchise landmark

A knowledgeable Marvel fan should now have a strong chance of identifying the real-world location.

### Clue 4 — Explicit answer

State the exact real-world location.

For example:

"The location is Trilith Studios."

"The location is Avengers Campus at Disney California Adventure."

"The location is the Atlanta City Hall East / former Sears building used for MCU production."

Clue 4 MUST explicitly identify the real-world answer. Prefer a clear public place name a player can look up and pin.

### Clue 5 — Geographical locating clue

Now help the player physically find the answer on the map.

For example:

"The location is in Fayetteville, Georgia, United States."

"The location is in Anaheim, California, United States."

"The location is in Atlanta, Georgia, United States."

Clue 5 should make the geography easier but MUST NOT provide coordinates.

# 2. IMPORTANT: THIS IS ALREADY A MARVEL GAME

Do NOT waste clues establishing that Marvel is involved.

Bad clues include:

"This location is associated with a famous superhero franchise."

"A well-known comic-book film was shot here."

"This place has a connection to Marvel."

The player already knows that.

Every clue should provide useful information about **why this particular real-world place is connected to Marvel**.

Also:

Do NOT make the player pin a fictional place. If Clue 4 says "The location is Wakanda," that is wrong. Clue 4 must name the real Earth place.

# 3. WHAT COUNTS AS A VALID LOCATION?

Search Marvel's **real-world production and public-franchise geography** for meaningful places.

Focus on entertainment-geography categories such as filming locations, studios, theme parks, premiere venues, exhibitions, and other well-documented public franchise sites.

The core focus should be the **Marvel Cinematic Universe** (films and Disney+ / Marvel Television live-action series with documented real-world shoots), plus official parks and public franchise landmarks.

Older Fox / Sony Marvel films may be included **only** where the real-world connection is strong, well documented, and interesting enough for a specialist Marvel quiz. Prefer MCU / Disney park geography when deciding what to prioritise.

Potential categories include:

## Film and TV filming locations

Locations used for principal photography, second unit, or other documented filming across:

- MCU Phase One through current Phase films
- MCU Disney+ / live-action series (*WandaVision*, *Loki*, *Hawkeye*, *Moon Knight*, *Ms. Marvel*, *She-Hulk*, *Secret Invasion*, *Echo*, *Agatha*, *Daredevil* revival, etc.)
- anthology / specials with documented real-world shoots
- other officially produced Marvel live-action projects with strong documented geography

Potential targets include:

- cities, streets, plazas, bridges, skylines
- universities, museums, civic buildings
- deserts, forests, beaches, islands, mountains
- towns, villages, historic sites
- hotels and buildings used as sets
- specific named landmarks
- studio backlots only where they are a meaningful, documentable, pin-able place

However:

**Do NOT automatically create a question for every Manhattan street or Atlanta parking lot used for a day of second-unit work.**

The place must have a documented Marvel filming (or production) connection strong enough to support a good Pin5 question.

Prefer the most specific documented site (a named building, campus, plaza, park land, studio) over a whole city.

## Studios and production facilities

Include well-documented commercial / public-industry places such as:

- Trilith Studios (formerly Pinewood Atlanta)
- Shepperton / Pinewood / Leavesden / other UK studios used for MCU productions
- Manhattan Beach / other US studios with a clear Marvel connection
- Marvel Studios / Disney production campuses where the public connection is well documented and quiz-worthy

Only include these where the Marvel connection is meaningful enough for a fan quiz.

## Theme parks and attractions

Include official Marvel theme-park lands and attractions where the real-world site is pin-able, such as:

- Avengers Campus (Disneyland Resort / Disney California Adventure)

- Guardians of the Galaxy attractions (e.g. Mission: Breakout, Cosmic Rewind) where they are a clear distinct map target
- other official Disney / Universal Marvel attractions with a clear physical location and enough Marvel-specific material for five good clues

Treat each park's Marvel land (or major distinct attraction, where pin-able and quiz-worthy) as a distinct location if coordinates and clues can meaningfully differ.

---

## Premieres, exhibitions and public franchise events

Include places associated with:

- world / major premieres
- significant public exhibitions
- museum displays of Marvel costumes, props or art (where the venue connection is strong)
- official celebrations or franchise landmarks open to the public

Only include them where the geographical location is meaningful and the Marvel connection can support five good clues.

---

## Comic / publishing places (use carefully)

Places with a strong, well-documented public connection to Marvel's publishing history may be included if they work as map questions — for example historic Marvel offices or publicly documented comic landmarks — but only where sources are solid and the place is interesting enough.

Prefer film / TV / park / studio geography when in doubt.

Do not stretch thin comic-history associations into weak quiz items.

---

## Franchise landmarks and other public places

Other categories are permitted if the connection is:

- significant
- interesting
- geographically meaningful
- well documented
- suitable for a Marvel fan quiz

Do not add tenuous locations merely to increase the dataset size.

Every map target must be a real place suitable for inclusion in a general-audience geography quiz.

# 4. TARGET THE MOST INTERESTING GEOGRAPHICAL LEVEL

Always choose the most specific sensible map target.

For example:

Prefer:

**Trilith Studios**

rather than:

**Atlanta**

when the clues relate specifically to that production hub.

Prefer:

**Avengers Campus at Disney California Adventure**

rather than:

**Anaheim**

when the clues are about that specific land.

Prefer:

**a named New York landmark used on screen**

rather than:

**New York City**

when the scene connection is specific.

However, cities, towns, islands, regions, states or countries are acceptable where that genuinely represents the meaningful Marvel connection.

The target must correspond to where the player should actually place their pin — always on Earth.

---

# 5. AVOID DUPLICATE MAP TARGETS

I want as many **distinct real-world locations** as the evidence supports — not many pieces of Marvel trivia attached to a small number of cities.

Do not repeatedly use:

- Atlanta
- New York
- Los Angeles
- London
- etc.

as generic targets.

Different specific locations within the same city ARE acceptable.

For example, several Atlanta / Georgia locations could be included if each has:

- a distinct physical location;
- its own coordinates;
- a meaningful Marvel connection;
- substantially different clues.

Likewise, multiple London / LA / New York locations are acceptable if they are genuinely separate places.

Before writing the final dataset, deduplicate all coordinates and location identities.

If the target is a street or neighbourhood, choose an appropriate representative point relevant to the Marvel connection.

If the target genuinely is a city, an appropriate central coordinate may be used.

# 8. RESEARCH AND ACCURACY

Research the facts rather than relying solely on memory.

# 9. CLUE QUALITY

The clues are one of the most important parts of the dataset.

Avoid boring generic clues such as:

"Marvel filmed here."

"This is a city used in a superhero movie."

"This location is in America."

"A famous comic-book franchise shot scenes here."

Instead use interesting Marvel-specific facts.

Clue 1 and Clue 2 should reward Marvel knowledge (films, scenes, in-universe places, production trivia).

Clue 3 should significantly narrow the possibilities.

Clue 4 gives the real-world answer.

Clue 5 helps locate that answer geographically.

The progression should feel like:

**Marvel deep cut → recognisable franchise fact → strong narrowing clue → real-world answer → map help**

Each clue must add new information.

Do not simply reword the same fact five times.

A strong pattern often looks like:

1. obscure production / scene detail
2. clearer in-universe place, character set-piece or famous scene
3. film / series / park / city narrowing
4. explicit real-world place name
5. country / region locating help

---

# 10. EXAMPLE CLUE PATTERN

For a location such as Avengers Campus at Disney California Adventure, the pattern should resemble:

1. A difficult production / park / attraction fact tied to that specific place.
2. A more recognisable Marvel association (e.g. Avengers / Guardians presence at the land).
3. A strong clue involving the relevant park and region.
4. "The location is Avengers Campus at Disney California Adventure."
5. "The location is in Anaheim, California, United States."

Do NOT copy this blindly.

Research the actual facts and create the strongest progression possible for each individual location.

8. Have rumours and fan theories been excluded or clearly avoided?

22. Is this substantially different from other entries?
